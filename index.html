Casual · Godot · 2026

Finders Keepers

Role: Supervisor / Narrative Designer (team of 5)

Submission for the 2-week Comfy Game Jam Summer: 2026, in which I wrote and structured the NPC dialogue and questline system. The given limitation required "the instructions are a lie". To satisfy this, we built a story that encourages the player to interact with the world in a way that leads to negative consequences, and rewards players for engaging with the narrative. I also sanctioned meetings and ensured we stayed within the allotted scope of our project, keeping all members informed and assisting where I was needed.

  • Designed a morality-driven dialogue and quest system that considers various player interactions and branches the story accordingly.
  • Wrote over 8k words of dialogue, narration, and item descriptions.
  • Lead a team to publish a completed, final product within a short timespan.

Narrative Mystery · Twine · 2026

My Prison

Role: Solo Writer & Designer

A short interactive fiction piece built entirely in Twine about confronting an unknown kidnapper in an unfamiliar place. Designed with the intention to showcase an emotional narrative with branching dialogue.

  • Concocted a mystery to unravel within ~3k words, 67 dialogue passages, and multiple endings.
  • Self-published on itch.io as my first solo project.

ABOUT

About me

Hi, I'm Isaiah — a writer and producer with a soft spot for emotional narratives. I'm inspired by books and games of all types: Disco Elysium, Before Your Eyes, the Discworld series and The Phantom Tollbooth to name a few. When I'm not writing, you'll always find me reading and engaging with other people's stories to learn from their experiences.

Outside of video games I run tabletop games like D&D, Vaesen, and The Contract RPG. Telling improvised stories that evolve on the fly, and are tailored to the players, has always been a joy of mine.
